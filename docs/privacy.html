ThumbPlan – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

ThumbPlan is a gamified daily planner with alarms. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. In short: ThumbPlan has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1. Information we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, receive, store or sell any personal information. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send your data anywhere on its own.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Tasks and progress: titles, notes, dates, categories, colours, coins, streaks and purchases from the in-app shop (which uses virtual coins only, not real money).
  • Your name: the display name you type in, used only for greetings inside the app.
  • Photos: if you attach a photo to a task, you pick it with the Android photo picker and the app keeps a copy in its private storage.
  • Audio: if you choose a music file for a task's alarm, you pick it with the system file picker and a copy is kept in the app's private storage. The app never records audio.

This information never leaves your device unless you take one of the actions described in section 3.

3. Backups and sync (your choice)

  • Sync folder / manual backup: if you choose a folder (for example in Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or local storage) or export a backup file, ThumbPlan writes a backup ZIP file to that place. That file is then handled by the storage provider you picked, under its own privacy policy. We never have access to it.
  • Android device backup: if Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may back up the app's data to your Google account. Google handles this under its privacy policy.
  • Sharing: if you tap "Invite", the app opens Android's share sheet with an invitation text, and you choose where to send it.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications, exact alarms, full-screen alerts, display over other apps: used only to ring task alarms on time, including over the lock screen.
  • Run at startup: used to restore your alarms after a reboot or a time change.
  • Foreground service (media playback), wake lock, vibration: used to play the alarm sound and vibration while an alarm is ringing.

The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or advertising ID.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete all data at any time with Settings → Erase all data in the app, or by uninstalling the app. Because we do not store any of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To delete backups you created, delete the backup file from the folder you chose.

6. Children

ThumbPlan is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
